# Koe Remind Support

"Leaving in ten minutes." Koe Remind is a reminder app that tells you in a character's voice, at the time and a little before.

- Requires: iPhone with iOS 18 or later (alarms that ring in Silent mode need iOS 26.1 or later)
- Languages: English and Japanese (follows your iPhone's language)
- Account: none needed

## What it does

- Tells you aloud at the time, and minutes before
- Alarms that ring even in Silent mode (at the time)
- Once, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly and more, with holidays handled
- Snooze, templates and your own templates
- Voices from iPhone, VOICEVOX Nemo's nine voices and 32 VOICEVOX characters
- "Speak in character": a character says it in their own words, in Japanese
- The clock, the weather and your next reminder on the home screen
- The weather read aloud (tap the weather, or after you stop a wake-up alarm)

## FAQ

<details markdown="1">
<summary>My alarm didn't ring</summary>

Please check the following.

1. **iOS version**: alarms need iOS 26.1 or later. On earlier versions, every reminder comes as a notification
2. **Alarms and notifications allowed**: in the Settings app › Apps › Koe Remind, check that alarms and notifications are on. You can also see them in the app's Settings › About This App
3. **The reminder is on**: check its switch in the list. A "Once" reminder turns itself off after it rings
4. **Holidays**: a reminder set to skip holidays doesn't ring on them
5. **Not opened for a while**: the app schedules the next ones when you open it or stop an alarm. With Background App Refresh on, it keeps them scheduled even on days you don't open it
6. **Volume**: the alarm volume can differ from the ringer's. Check it with the volume buttons

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Does it ring in Silent mode?</summary>

- **Alarms** (at the time, or minutes before when you choose an alarm) speak even in Silent mode or a Focus, and repeat until you stop them (iOS 26.1 or later)
- **Notifications** (minutes before, by default) only vibrate in Silent mode, without the voice

For a reminder you want to hear in Silent mode, choose "Alarm" in its alert settings.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Can I stop the alarm vibrating?</summary>

iOS alarms vibrate until you stop them, with no setting to turn it off. Choose "Notification" for reminders you don't want to vibrate.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Using iPhone voices</summary>

- Choose one under "iPhone Voices" in the voice list, sorted by country with yours first
- Add higher-quality voices in the Settings app: Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices. Reopen the app and they appear in the list
- iPhone voices don't speak in character

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>VOICEVOX voices and downloads</summary>

- VOICEVOX voices are Japanese. With the app in English, they read English with a Japanese accent
- ずんだもん, 四国めたん, 春日部つむぎ and 雨晴はう come with the app. Other voices (styles) download when you choose them
- Each file is about 60 MB
- Once downloaded, they speak offline
- If a download fails, check your connection and try again. Without the voice's data, the reminder rings with the standard sound

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>The weather doesn't show</summary>

- Without location access, choose "Choose a Place" in the app's Settings › Weather › Place and search for a city
- Offline, it shows the weather it got last. If it has never got any, the weather doesn't show
- The weather comes from Apple Weather. Weather warnings are read in Japan only

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Which holidays does it know?</summary>

The app knows Japan's holidays. In Settings › Clock › Holidays, choose "Device setting" (Japan's holidays when your iPhone's region is Japan, none elsewhere), "None" or "Japan".

Japan's holidays come with the app as published by the Cabinet Office (currently through 2027). Later years follow the current holiday law and the equinox formulas. Changes in the law or one-off holidays come with app updates.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>How do I delete my data?</summary>

Your reminders and settings are kept only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases them all; nothing is left on our servers (usage statistics are deleted under the Google Analytics settings).

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>What information is sent?</summary>

To improve the app, how its features are used (the kinds of reminder settings, the kind of voice, template use and so on) is sent to Google Analytics for Firebase as statistics that don't identify you. Reminder titles and your location are never sent.

To stop it, turn off "Share Usage Data" in the app's Settings › Other › About This App. See "6. Usage analytics" in the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/) for details.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Can I move my data to a new iPhone?</summary>

There's no syncing such as iCloud. Restoring a whole-iPhone backup may bring it along.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Does it cost anything?</summary>

The app is free to download, and every voice is free to use. The free version shows ads.

Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription) adds:

- No ads
- More than 5 reminders and more than 1 template of your own
- A voice for each reminder
- Styles other than Normal (whispering, cheerful and more)
- Speak in character

To cancel, go to the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

</details>
